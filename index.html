Portfolio projects

1. Higher Education Strategy and Transformation

An end-to-end insight and strategy pack built from one anonymised admissions dataset: raw data, to a Power BI dashboard, to a 17-slide insight and strategy deck. The analysis traces 62,180 applicants to 7,415 enrolments, finds where conversion is lost by fee status, level, school and course, benchmarks growth against the sector with HESA data, and sets out five moves with owners and measures.
